ADMISSION STATUS

Admissions Are Currently Closed

Our current admission window has closed. Families interested in joining Royal Crest International School are encouraged to check back for the next admission period.

Admission dates, available places and application information will be announced when the next admission window opens.

JOIN OUR COMMUNITY

A Thoughtful Start to Every Learner's Journey.

We believe choosing a school is an important decision for every family. Our admission process is designed to help parents understand our learning environment, values and expectations before joining our community.

HOW IT WORKS

Our Admission Process

When admissions reopen, families can expect a clear and straightforward process.

01

Make an Enquiry

Parents and guardians can contact the school to learn about available places, school programmes and admission dates.

02

Submit Application

Families complete the required admission information and provide the necessary documents for consideration.

03

Family Meeting

The school may invite parents and learners for a meeting or assessment appropriate to the learner's level.

04

Admission Decision

Successful applicants receive information about the next steps for completing their enrolment.

REQUIREMENTS

What Families May Need

Requirements may vary depending on the learner's age and level of entry. The following documents may be requested during the admission process.

Completed admission information
Recent passport photographs
Previous school records where applicable
Birth certificate or age declaration
Relevant medical or health information
QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

When will admissions reopen?

Admissions are currently closed. The next admission window and available places will be announced when registration reopens.

Can I still contact the school?

Yes. Families can contact the school using the contact details provided on this website for general enquiries.

Which year groups can apply?

Our programmes cover Early Years, Primary School and Secondary School. Availability depends on the current admission cycle.

Is admission guaranteed after applying?

No. Admission is subject to available places and the school's admission requirements and assessment process.

STAY CONNECTED

Planning for the Next Admission Window?

Contact us for general information and enquiries about Royal Crest International School.
